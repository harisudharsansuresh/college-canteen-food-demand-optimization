Expected profit is the sum of each scenario's profit multiplied by its probability. The shortage penalty is a user-defined proxy for service impact, not necessarily a direct cash expense.

04 · How the model works

The model calculates sales, leftovers and shortages for every demand scenario.

Profit = Sales revenue + Leftover recovery − Preparation cost − Shortage penalty

Expected profit = Σ (Scenario probability × Scenario profit)

The optimizer tests each whole-meal preparation quantity from zero up to the entered capacity and selects the one with the highest expected profit.
